What is VARK?

VARK is a learning styles model by Neil Fleming from New Zealand. It groups the ways people prefer to take in information into four styles: Visual (diagrams, charts), Aural (listening, discussion), Read / Write (reading text, writing notes), and Kinesthetic (doing, practicing). No style is better than another — most people are a mix of several.

How to use your result?

  • Learn new things the way that fits you: Visual learners draw more, Aural learners talk it out, Read/Write learners take notes, Kinesthetic learners practice right away.
  • Combining styles works even better: look at a diagram first, explain it to someone, then do it yourself.
  • To go deeper into “you only learn it once you can explain it”, try the Feynman Technique: teach what you learned to someone else — if you can’t explain it, you haven’t learned it.
