What is the Pomodoro Technique?

The Pomodoro Technique is simple: focus for 25 minutes, then take a 5-minute break — that is one Pomodoro. After every 4 Pomodoros, take a longer 15-minute break. Short, fixed rhythms make it easier to concentrate and harder to burn out.

How to use it?

  1. Press Start and do just one thing for 25 minutes. Put your phone aside.
  2. When time is up, a popup appears and a sound plays — even if you switched to another tab, the browser notification pops up. Stand up, walk around, drink some water, and rest for 5 minutes.
  3. After 4 Pomodoros, take yourself a 15-minute long break.
  4. Your daily Pomodoro count and focus minutes are saved automatically — closing the page won’t lose them.
