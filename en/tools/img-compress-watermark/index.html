    What is Image Compress + Watermark?

    Before posting images to your blog or social media, shrink them, resize as needed, and add a watermark — all in one go. Images are processed only in your browser and never uploaded to any server.

    How to use it?

    1. Drag images into the box, or click to choose them — you can select several at once.
    2. Set the compression quality, output format, and max width/height above, then fill in the watermark text, position, and opacity.
    3. Each image shows a before/after size comparison — click Download to save it. To try different settings, change them and click “Re-process all with current settings”.
