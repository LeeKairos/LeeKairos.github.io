What is the DDL Doc Generator?

When writing docs, handing over a database, or doing code review, you often need a readable description of your tables. This tool parses MySQL CREATE TABLE statements into tables showing columns, types, nullability, defaults, comments, primary keys, and indexes at a glance — and exports them as Markdown. Everything is parsed locally in your browser; your DDL is never uploaded.

How to use it?

  1. Paste CREATE TABLE statements into the box on the left — several tables at once if you like. Or click “Load Sample” to see how it works.
  2. Click “Parse & Generate Docs” and the tables appear on the right.
  3. Click “Export Markdown” to save a .md file, or “Copy Markdown” to paste it straight into your docs.
